## Before publishing or submitting

If you replace a portrait with art, a photo, or a render, use work you created or have permission to use, or verify the exact license on the asset's source page. Do not treat a Google Images result as permission. Record the creator, original source URL, license, and any required attribution here. Update the character's `portrait` and `alt` fields in `src/data/characters.json` and place local files under `public/`.

This credits file documents the assets currently present; it is not a legal determination of their rights status. Follow the college's submission rules and ask the original creator about any unclear asset provenance.
